# Healthcare KPI Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes healthcare data to identify patterns in patient distribution, medical conditions, billing amounts, hospital workload, and length of stay. The goal is to extract meaningful insights that can support healthcare planning and decision-making.

---

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## Key KPIs Analyzed

### 1. Patient Distribution
- Analyzed number of patients across medical conditions
- Identified most common diseases in the dataset

### 2. Billing Analysis
- Calculated average billing amount per medical condition
- Identified high-cost conditions such as Cancer and Heart Disease

### 3. Hospital Workload
- Evaluated patient distribution across hospitals
- Found Northwestern Memorial Hospital as the highest workload facility

### 4. Length of Stay
- Analyzed average hospital stay per medical condition
- Identified Alzheimer’s and Cancer as long-duration conditions

---

## Key Insights

- Cancer and Heart Disease are the most expensive conditions in terms of treatment cost.
- Alzheimer’s has the longest average hospital stay, indicating long-term care requirements.
- Flu and Asthma have low cost and short hospital stays.
- Patient distribution is relatively balanced across major hospitals.
- Healthcare demand varies significantly across medical conditions in terms of cost, frequency, and severity.

---

## Visualization
The project includes:
- Bar charts for KPI analysis
- Hospital workload visualization
- Heatmap combining cost, patient count, and length of stay

---

## Conclusion
This analysis provides a multi-dimensional view of healthcare data, helping identify high-cost conditions, resource-heavy diseases, and hospital workload distribution. These insights can support better healthcare planning and decision-making.
